# Accounting Analyst Improvement Backlog

This backlog tracks process-improvement ideas, tool opportunities, and
streamlining recommendations identified by the Accounting Analyst.

Items here are recommendations only. Building tools, removing tools, changing
production systems, changing QuickBooks records, changing authentication, or
changing accounting policy requires explicit human approval and must stay inside
the Accounting Analyst RRE.

Do not store credentials, raw invoice rows, customer private data, production
database details, or sensitive operational details in this file.

## Evaluation Lens

Use this lens when reviewing a process:

- Value: what human time, error risk, delay, or uncertainty would this reduce?
- Capability: what local tool, export, report, checklist, or integration would
  be needed?
- Knowledge: what source data, rules, and evidence would be gathered or stored?
- Decision rights: what can the Accounting Analyst recommend versus what stays
  with the human?
- Feedback: what metric or signal would show the change worked?
