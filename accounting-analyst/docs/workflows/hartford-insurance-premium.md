# Hartford Insurance Premium

This note documents the learned status for AM-015.

Do not store policy numbers, invoice copies, bank details, credentials, or
sensitive insurance/payment details in this file.

## Current Status

- Active monthly verification/documentation task.
- Not a manual payment task.

## Payment Handling

Hartford is set up to auto-draft from checking on the 10th of the month.

## Reminder Handling

Use two reminder/control moments:

- Around the 10th: verify the auto-draft occurred.
- Around the 20th: download the invoice, which is usually generated around that
  time.

## Completion Check

AM-015 is complete when the auto-draft has been verified and the invoice has
been downloaded/stored in the approved accounting records location.
